## Sari-Sari Store Inventory System

### 1. Encapsulation
Some parts of the previous simple inventory system of the sari-sari store may be retained and integrated to further 
simplify and organize the program. For instance, the item information, which includes its name and price. Instead of 
creating various variables to store the information needed, we can use classes or simple templates to help guide the 
data being stored. 

### 2. Abstraction
Developing a system which organizes the sari-sari store's processes doesn't need to involve the full details of each step. 
Include only the essential parts, which are the simple yet suggestive operations such as adding items, removing items, and 
displaying the items. In this way, the buyers will understand each process in an easier manner.

### 3. Inheritance
The inheritance concept allows the attainment of existing properties and methods from the parent object. In this case, the process 
assigned to story important information may be expanded to be able to accomodate and categorized food and beverages. Through this 
method, the processess required to run a functional sari-sari store may be more manageable for most.

### 4. Polymorphism
From the expansion of categorized items through inheritance, the concept of polmorphism may also be applied here. Here, 
information store and displaya actions may be categorized to conform such processes to the two classifications of edible supplies,
the food and the beverages.
